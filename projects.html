PAN document quality & field verification

Prototyped a workflow combining image-quality checks, information extraction, validation and decision logic. In a 30-document synthetic comparison, OpenBharatOCR extracted the PAN number correctly in 29 cases, while Qwen extracted all four required fields correctly in 22 cases versus 10 for OpenBharatOCR — a useful reminder to measure full-record correctness as well as individual fields.

Methods: OpenBharatOCR · Qwen · image preprocessing · rule-based checks
