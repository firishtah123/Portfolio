I studied Geographic Information Science before moving deeper into geographic data science, statistical modelling and machine learning. That foundation shaped how I approach data: not as isolated numbers, but as evidence connected to places, systems and people.

Since then, my work has moved across ESG analytics, academic research and banking technology. I have worked with emissions and reporting data, combined satellite and socioeconomic datasets for climate research, and tested AI-based approaches to document processing. The subject changes; the discipline stays the same — prepare the data carefully, choose a method that fits the question, test its limits and communicate the evidence clearly.

I’m interested in opportunities across data science, AI and risk analytics, including finance and consulting environments where analytical rigour, model validation, operational awareness and a geographic perspective can be useful. I bring hands-on experience, curiosity about how models behave in practice, and a willingness to look closely at what the data can and cannot support.

Aug 2026 — Present · Mumbai, India

HDFC Bank

Project Trainee, Data Scientist — GenAI & Non-GenAI Solutions

At HDFC Bank, I work on the Intelligent Document Retrieval System (IDRS), exploring how OCR engines and vision-language models process identity and financial documents. My work spans document classification, information extraction, field-level validation, document-quality assessment and structured-output checks. I benchmark approaches against labelled ground truth, comparing accuracy and robustness alongside inference constraints and practical deployment considerations. I have evaluated open-source options including Qwen-family vision-language models and Gemma, alongside OCR-based approaches, and investigated how schema validation and human review can strengthen extraction workflows. This work is experimental and task-specific; results are interpreted against the evaluated dataset rather than presented as general production accuracy.

Research Assistant
