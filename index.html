THE CURRENT CHAPTER

Making AI
earn its keep.

HDFC Bank · Tech & Digital · GenAI and Non-GenAI Solutions Team

At HDFC Bank, I work on IDRS — the Intelligent Document Retrieval System — exploring how AI can turn documents like PAN cards and bank statements into structured, useful information. The interesting part isn’t getting a model to produce an answer. It’s finding out whether that answer is correct, consistent, affordable and good enough for the job.

I’ve compared open-source vision-language models with proprietary options, built document-processing prototypes, tested OCR, explored fine-tuning, and worked through the unglamorous details: image quality, schema validation, transaction reconciliation, GPU memory and the errors that look tiny until they affect a financial record.

A confident-looking answer is not the same thing as a correct one. I build my evaluations around that distinction.
