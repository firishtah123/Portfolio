Risk & Climate Analytics

Flood Risk & Climate Vulnerability Modelling

Modelled flood exposure across 4,835 London LSOAs by combining hazard rasters with socioeconomic and demographic indicators. The project uses spatial statistics and modelling to examine how physical hazards intersect with social vulnerability.

METHODS & TOOLSQGIS · Python · Spatial ML · Geospatial econometrics

The question

The analytical question is how flood hazard and socioeconomic vulnerability vary across small geographic areas, and how combining these data sources can support a more contextual view of risk.

Data

Hazard rasters, socioeconomic and demographic indicators, and London LSOA boundaries.

Method

Spatial data integration, raster analysis, statistical modelling and geographic comparison.

Findings & evidence
