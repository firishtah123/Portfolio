Satellite Remote Sensing & Environmental Risk

Analysed geographic variation in NO₂ exposure across Greater London during 2019–2020 using Sentinel-5P satellite data and Google Earth Engine.

METHODS & TOOLSGoogle Earth Engine · Sentinel-5P · Remote sensing

The question

This project examines how satellite observations can be used to investigate patterns in atmospheric pollution and compare exposure geographically.

Data

Sentinel-5P observations for Greater London, 2019–2020.

Method
