A LITTLE CONTEXT

MUMBAI · LONDON · SINGAPORE

I like working where
different systems meet.

My background is in Geographic Information Science and Geographic Data Science, where I learned to connect data to the physical and social worlds in which it exists. That perspective continues to shape how I approach analytical problems: understand the data, understand its context, test the assumptions, and make the result useful.

At the London School of Economics, I worked with satellite imagery, flood-hazard data and socioeconomic indicators to examine climate exposure and geographic vulnerability. At HDFC Bank, I now work on financial-document intelligence and model evaluation—testing how extraction systems behave, how outputs can be validated, and where models fail.

Before that, at Project Flux, I worked across ESG data and analytics, contributing to environmental reporting and leading a cross-functional team. Across these roles, the common thread is turning messy, multidimensional data into something more structured, explainable and decision-ready.
