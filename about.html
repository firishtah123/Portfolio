A QUICK INTRODUCTION

I didn’t pick one kind of problem. I picked the habit of figuring things out.

My background is in Geographic Information Science and Geographic Data Science, which taught me to look at data in context: where it came from, what it misses, and who might be affected by the conclusions. Since then, my work has moved between climate and environmental research, ESG analytics, and applied AI.

At HDFC Bank, I’ve brought that same mindset to document intelligence. I’m testing models on real task requirements, comparing open-source options with proprietary systems, and looking beyond a polished output to the reliability underneath it. At the London School of Economics, I worked with large geospatial datasets to study flood risk, inequality and environmental change.

I like ambitious questions, clear evidence, practical solutions — and people who are happy to say, “I don’t know yet. Let’s test it.”

Outside the technical bits, I care about being a good collaborator, communicating clearly and keeping a sense of humour when the code inevitably decides to have a personality of its own.

Stay curious.

Follow the question, not the hype.

Be rigorous.

Measure it. Check it. Try to break it.

Keep it human.

Useful work starts with real people.
