About Firishtah

A journey across disciplines.

I studied Geographic Information Science before moving deeper into geographic data science, statistical modelling and machine learning. That foundation gave me a way to think about data in context: where patterns occur, how systems interact and what might be missed when information is viewed in isolation.

My experience has since moved across ESG analytics, research and banking technology. I have worked with emissions data and reporting, combined satellite and socioeconomic datasets for climate research, and evaluated AI-based approaches to document processing.

These are different domains, but the work shares a common thread: preparing data carefully, choosing methods that fit the question, testing results and communicating what the evidence does — and does not — support.

Today, I’m interested in roles at the intersection of data science, AI and risk, including work where quantitative and geospatial thinking can add a useful perspective.
